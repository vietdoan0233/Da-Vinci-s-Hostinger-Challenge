Decode noise

Research prototype. Paste a short English text to see which sentences match persuasion techniques.
What this tool does not do
  • It does not say whether a statement is true or false. It is not a fact-checker.
  • It does not infer intent, honesty or affiliation, and does not say who is right.
  • "No technique flagged" is not "neutral".
  • HTTP is not encrypted. Do not paste personal or confidential text.
